# E12 · Field-term dip vs firing rate as a marker of peak acceleration: results

> The **results write-up**. Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_2026-10-12.md` (amendments A1–A3, all committed before any real-data computation; the real-data run used commit `3bd7f2a`, which contains A3).
> Runs are cited by the timestamp in `runs/RUN_LOG.md`. **P** = primary real-data run (2026-10-12 20:36), **S** = sensitivity runs (20:38–20:46), **V** = synthetic method validation (20:32). n = 21 sessions (63 conditions, reaches with `exclude_k == 0`), two-sided Wilcoxon, BH-FDR over the 12-test primary family, 95% CIs by session bootstrap.

## Summary (plain language)
The field-term dip does line up with peak acceleration in real data (trough in the 200 ms before peak velocity in 65% of conditions vs 14% in rest controls), and it is far more precise than the population-average rate (median error 50 ms vs 330 ms). But it is **not unique to the Ising model**. The uncoupled model's field term (FT0) and a model-free polarization index (POLb) give the same timing, and we cannot detect any advantage of the coupling-corrected fields (FT vs FT0: T1 +0.02, CI −0.10 to +0.15; T2 −20 ms, CI −59 to +12 ms; equivalence is not shown, because the CIs are wider than the ±0.10 / ±15 ms margins). The dip is best read as a population **polarization** effect: cells move away from 50/50 in both directions, so the mean rate cancels but the field term does not. A supervised decoder trained on peak acceleration is more precise still (20 ms); it is a ceiling for rate readouts, not a competitor on equal terms. The "4.7x deeper than control" depth figure from E09 is inflated by the spline basis and should be dropped.

## Methods-deviation table (where our method differs from standard ones)
| Method | Finds the dip near peak acceleration? | Timing (median error to peak acc.) | What it cannot say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field term, M1 (ours) | Yes, 65% vs 14% control | 50 ms | Does not beat FT0 or POLb on timing; depth advantage is basis-driven |
| Field term, M0 (independent) | Yes, 64% | 70 ms | Cannot link the dip to coupling influence |
| Model-free polarization (POLb, dFR) | Yes, 62% / 56% | 70 / 100 ms | No energy split, no J or susceptibility (not tested here) |
| Population-average rate (FR) | Weakly: dips, but 30% in window | 330 ms | Bidirectional changes cancel, so its timing is imprecise |
| PCA trajectory speed (dPCA reduces to PCA here) | Partly, 41% | 100 ms | Speed peaks at the flanks of a bump, not its centre; distance from baseline is worse (190 ms behind FT) |
| Supervised decoder (DEC) | Yes, 91% | 20 ms | Needs the peak-acceleration label; ceiling, not unsupervised detection |
| GPFA | NOT RUN (no validated implementation here) | - | - |
| Calame pooled velocity-binned rate (POOL) | NOT RUN (needs hand kinematics: sbatch not permitted in this session, pending the user's decision) | - | - |

What is unique to the model is therefore not the dip's detection. The remaining candidate contributions (coupling-corrected fields in E10, the link of polarization to coupling influence P4, and the correct split of reach energy into h vs J in B2) are not tested by E12.

## Caveats and open issues
- **Provenance of the synthetic validation (V, 2026-10-12 20:32).** It was logged at commit e5d0027 "+uncommitted changes in experiment"; the code it ran was committed in 781c187 three minutes later. The validation outputs in `assets/tables/synthetic_*.csv` come from that code.
- **Preliminary figure.** `assets/figures/prelim_field_vs_rate_trough.*` (`code/make_pi_figure.py`) is superseded by `T1_T2_by_signal.png`; the prelim depth panel is basis-inflated (R7).
- **POOL and the rest-control check are NOT RUN.** Both need unscaled per-reach kinematics, not in `spikes_v2.npz`. The extraction job (`code/extract_kinematics.py`, `code/sbatch_kin.sh`) was prepared; `sbatch` was denied in this session (shared-cluster mutation). Blocked, pending the user's decision. Until then the "rest" status of the control windows (−1.5/−2/−2.5 s) is assumed, not verified. The 16 GB raw-file copy in `~/ising_wt/e12/raw` is to be deleted after the job. If this step is completed, add it as a dated amendment and update this file.
- U1 and U2 are **unresolved, not proven equal**. With 21 sessions the CIs are wider than the registered tie margins, so we report "no detectable advantage", never "identical".
- The primary family has 12 tests. FT vs FR on T1 does not survive FDR (p_BH = 0.078) even though the CI excludes 0; T2 does (p_BH = 0.024). Do not state the T1 difference as significant.
- The prior-look numbers (60% vs 14%, 70 vs 210 ms) were exploratory; the registered recomputation gives 65% vs 14%, 50 ms vs 330 ms. The exploratory depth ratio (4.7x vs 2.0x) is not used.
- DEC trains on the same search window it is evaluated on (disjoint from the control windows); its 91% is an upper bound for rate readouts.
- Field fits are pseudo-likelihood (M1); in strongly coupled 2022 sessions it fails moment matching (HANDOFF §5). The field term uses only the fields, but this could matter for U1 in 2022 (n = 6).
- Smoothing, the extremum search and the baseline normalisation are part of the definition: results on the dip's depth depend on them (S shows timing results are stable).
