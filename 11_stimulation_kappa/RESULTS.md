## Summary (plain language)
We asked whether the closed-loop laser pulse changes how Purkinje cells interact, or only how much they fire. We measure one number per session, κ, the scale on the coupling matrix in the 60 ms after laser onset; the quantity tested is the difference between that and the same reach phase in baseline reaches (stim_0).
**The pre-registered test is inconclusive (outcome c).** Across 21 sessions the raw difference leans negative (median of sessions −0.12; Hodges–Lehmann −0.27; 90% CI −0.47 to −0.02), but it does not survive correction (Wilcoxon p = 0.065, FDR 0.13) and the interval is too wide to call coupling "unchanged" within ±0.3. A second, model-based measure of coupling influence, the multi-information per neuron, does not change in the pulse window (p = 0.79).
So we can say neither that stimulation changes coupling nor that it leaves coupling alone. If anything the data lean toward a decrease, and the validation shows our estimator tends to under-state a true decrease (see Caveats). The lean is not stable to the choice of control window (a fixed 401–406 control gives −0.09, p = 0.41) and it is carried by the 2021 cohort.

## Caveats and open issues
1. **What went wrong first, and what changed (full account).** The first full run (run 1) fitted bins 0–699 instead of the pre-registered 300–559, wrote laser onsets at 10 ms precision, and used a shuffle null that misaligned each neuron's response with its window. An independent code review found these; the results were retracted (`RESULTS_run1_SUPERSEDED.md`), amendment A3 was written before any refit, and the validation was redone with the real pipeline (runs 6, 6b). That validation showed the null-corrected statistic failed its criteria in both onset-jitter groups, so amendment A4 (approved by Gunnar) made the raw Δκ the primary statistic, with the shuffle null as a diagnostic only. A5 encoded the decision rule and the handling of incomplete data after a second review of the analysis script, before the refit outputs were opened. Run-1 and refit headline numbers are close (raw HL −0.233 vs −0.266, both inconclusive).
2. **Disclosure (seen-before sessions and choices).** Sessions 210511 (smoke test) and 220519 (cost timing) were fitted on real data before the full runs. The primary change from run 1 to the refit (raw instead of null-corrected statistic) was decided after the run-1 numbers were seen, on synthetic-validation grounds; in run 1 raw and corrected agreed. R4 reports S1 without the two seen sessions.
3. **Estimator limits from validation (these bias toward zero for a decrease).** A rate change confined to the window leaks *upward* by +0.17 to +0.25, and a rate ramp lowers the recovery of a true effect to 0.28–0.33 of its size. Both push a true *decrease* toward zero, so the observed lean is, if anything, understated; an *increase* would have to be read with that leak in mind. Single-session precision is poor (shuffle-null sd of κ per window about 0.57, larger than the 0.2–0.5 in the synthetic sessions), and sessions are weighted equally.
4. **Interpret only the pulse-minus-control difference, never κ_pulse alone.** Absolute κ in a window depends on the field regularization and on reach phase. The earlier regularization table (run 1) was computed under the wrong fitted range and is retired; the dependence was **not** re-tested on the refit.
5. **Control-window dependence (R3).** The primary control draws a phase for each stim_0 reach from the stim_1 onset distribution; the fixed control gives a smaller, non-significant lean. We do not know which is closer to "the same reach phase". Both were pre-registered.
6. **Nominal sensitivity p-values (R4, R5).** Four rows reach p < 0.05 (all reaches, N ≥ 5, leave-out, 2021 cohort). They are descriptive, uncorrected, and not the pre-registered outcome; they share most of their data with the primary, and the cohort split was not a pre-registered contrast. Protocol differences between cohorts are unconfirmed.
7. **Opsin status unconfirmed.** Whether all 6 mice are opsin-positive is an open lab question; there is no opsin-negative or red-light negative control in this analysis. A negative control would test whether the 2021 lean is stimulation-specific.
8. **Scope.** One κ for all pairs in a 60-ms window; fits cover bins 300–559 only, N ≤ 16 neurons per fit (10 random subsets when N > 16; the one full exact check agrees), cell stability across blocks not checked, putative PCs included as exported. 12 sessions have N > 16.
9. **No claim of invariance and no claim of change.** The equivalence criterion (90% CI inside ±0.3) is not met for S1; only the fixed-control variant meets a CI inside ±0.3 (R3), and that is a sensitivity analysis.
