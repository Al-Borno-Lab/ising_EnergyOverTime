# E11 · Stimulation model: does optogenetic perturbation change coupling (kappa)?: results

> Each claim cites its asset and the run (see `runs/RUN_LOG.md`). Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_2026-10-12.md` (+ amendments A1, A2). Method validation (synthetic): `VALIDATION_CRITERIA_2026-10-12.md`.
> Full run: jobs 4363060 (A), 4363061 (B), 4363062 (C), exactness check 4363063, regularization table 4363370; code commit `25c1136`.

## Summary (plain language)
We asked whether the closed-loop laser pulse changes how Purkinje cells interact, or only how much they fire. We measured this with one number per session, κ, the scale on the coupling matrix in the 60 ms after the laser onset, compared with the same reach phase in baseline reaches (stim_0).
**The pre-registered primary test is inconclusive.** Across 21 sessions the pulse-minus-control difference leans negative (median −0.24; 90% CI −0.44 to −0.03), but the test does not survive correction (Wilcoxon p = 0.070, FDR-adjusted 0.14), and the interval is too wide to call coupling "unchanged" within the pre-registered ±0.3. The multi-information per neuron (a second, model-based measure of coupling influence) does not change in the pulse window (p = 0.97). We therefore cannot say that stimulation changes coupling, and we cannot say it leaves coupling invariant.
Two features of the data limit what can be said: the answer depends on how the control window is defined (a fixed 401–406 control gives −0.08, consistent with zero), and the sessions split by cohort (2021 negative, 2022 positive).

## Caveats and open issues
1. **Disclosure (seen-before sessions and setting choices).** Sessions 210511 (smoke test) and 220519 (cost timing) were fitted on real data before the full run, so their κ values were seen; 210511's primary Δκ was about 0. The optimiser tolerance (1e-9 → 1e-7) and the rejection of a stronger spline ridge (λ_B 1e-4) were chosen with real data in view, on iteration counts and log-likelihood, **not** on κ or Δκ. The full run refit these two sessions; R5 reports S1 without them. See pre-registration amendment A2.
2. **Interpret only the pulse-minus-control difference, never κ_pulse alone.** The absolute κ in a window depends strongly on how the fields are regularized (210511: −0.34 vs 0.02 under λ_B 1e-5 vs 1e-4) and on the window's reach phase; the difference between two windows fitted in the same model is much more stable (R9). Differences smaller than about 0.1 in |κ| are not interpretable (synthetic rate-only bias +0.08).
3. **Real-data noise is larger than the validation suggested.** Single-session shuffle-null spread of κ is 0.04 (N ≤ 5) to about 0.7 (several 2022 and large-N sessions), and subset-to-subset spread of Δκ in the 12 sessions with N > 16 is 0.07–0.60. Synthetic session-sized data gave a single-session sd of 0.12–0.15. Sessions are weighted equally in the Wilcoxon test, so a few noisy sessions can move it. Report the interval, not only p.
4. **Control-window dependence (R3).** The primary control draws a phase for every stim_0 reach from the stim_1 onset distribution. The fixed 401–406 control gives a smaller effect. We do not know which is closer to "the same reach phase": we only know the conclusion depends on this choice. Both were pre-registered.
5. **Cohort split (R6).** The 2021 vs 2022 contrast is a post-hoc lead. Protocol differences between cohorts are unconfirmed (2022 sessions are not in the preprint, and the lab has not confirmed how they were recorded).
6. **Opsin status unconfirmed.** Whether all 6 mice are opsin-positive is still an open lab question. No sessions were identified as opsin-negative or red-light controls, so there is no negative control in this analysis. An opsin-negative mouse would be a natural control for the negative 2021 effect.
7. **Scope of the model.** κ is a single scale on all pairs in a 60-ms window; pair-specific changes are not tested. Fits use bins 300–559 only, N ≤ 16 neurons per fit (10 random subsets when N > 16; the one full exact check agrees), and the unit-stability of cells across blocks was not checked. Putative PCs are included as exported.
8. **No claim of invariance.** The equivalence criterion (90% CI inside ±0.3) was not met for S1 in the primary analysis. Only under the fixed-control variant is it met (R3), and that variant is a sensitivity analysis.
